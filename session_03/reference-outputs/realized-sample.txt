Session 3 - Food Ontology Realization
EquivalentTo vs SubClassOf

Experiment 1: EquivalentTo
  product_sesame_1: SesameDeclarationCandidate, BrandedFood
  product_sesame_2: SesameDeclarationCandidate, BrandedFood
  product_other_1: BrandedFood

Experiment 2: SubClassOf
  product_sesame_1: BrandedFood
